Built around your catalog files

  • Free, no subscription The apps are free and open source (MIT). No accounts, no plans.
  • Your catalog is one file Images, hotspots and data live together in a single .ecatm file on your device. It goes nowhere unless you share it.
  • Photos and diagrams Put hotspots on any image — a room photo, a parts diagram, an anatomy chart — and link images together: tap the sofa in the room to open its close-up.
  • Links to your own store Point items at any online store you already use. There's no marketplace or checkout of ours in between.
  • Works offline after you first open it After you first open the apps on a computer, tablet or phone, they are cached in your browser and keep working without a connection.
  • Live editing and catalog sharing, only if you want them Two optional apps you run yourself: the Collaboration Server for live editing together, and the Catalog Server for sharing a folder of catalogs on your network or the internet. Everything else works without them.

Collaboration Server downloads

Run the Collaboration Server yourself to edit a catalog live with other people. You don't need it to create or view a catalog on your own.

Windows: Get it on Microsoft Store (or direct download, .exe file)

Linux: Get it on Snap Store (Ubuntu App Center, or direct download for other distros)

macOS: (requires macOS 13+) direct download (Apple Silicon, or Intel) — unzip, then see the release page for the one-line Terminal command needed on first run
